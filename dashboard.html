📜 Points Rules

How Points Are Calculated

Total Points =
  (Eligible Ideas Watched × 10)
+ (Verified Referrals × 300)
+ (Approved Original Ideas × 100)

📺 Watching Ideas — 10 Points Each

  • Watch any eligible idea posted during the challenge period.
  • Each unique idea counts once per user — repeated views earn no extra points.
  • Only ideas approved by ManIdea admin are eligible.

👥 Referring Friends — 300 Points Each

  • Share your unique referral link with friends.
  • Points awarded only after the referred user is verified by admin.
  • Self-referrals are not allowed and will be disqualified.
  • Duplicate referral rewards are prevented automatically.

💡 Posting Ideas — 100 Points Each

  • Post original, creative ideas during the challenge period.
  • Points awarded only after admin approves the idea.
  • Duplicate, plagiarised, or rejected ideas earn no points.

🚫 Disqualification Rules

  • Fake accounts or bot activity → permanent disqualification.
  • Buying or selling referrals → disqualification.
  • Manipulating view counts → disqualification.
  • Any form of cheating → removal from leaderboard.
